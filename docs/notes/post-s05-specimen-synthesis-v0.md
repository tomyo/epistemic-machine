# Post-S05 Specimen Synthesis v0

**Status:** bounded evidence synthesis — not roadmap adoption, a new experiment, a stable interface, a shared foundation, or runtime architecture selection

## Purpose and disposition

This review exercises the exploration exit criterion in the [candidate incremental embodiment roadmap](../roadmaps/incremental-embodiment-roadmap-v0.md). It audits the completed evidence records—not a retrospective design conversation—to decide whether another specimen now tests a materially new relationship.

**Disposition:** no such relationship is presently exposed. **No S06 is selected. No helper is promoted.** The current specimen branch pauses for synthesis rather than accumulating another variation. A future specimen needs a **materially new observed relationship** or **real machine-ecology workflow pressure**. A candidate-boundary specification, if warranted, is a separately authorized experiment rather than a consequence of this review.

## Evidence audit

| Record | Bounded support | What remains unearned |
|---|---|---|
| [M00](../missions/m00-first-continuity-loop.md) | One experiment-local accepted consequence and its provenance were reconstructed after an operator replacement. | A reusable continuity substrate, a general session model, or root `.em/` state. |
| [S03](../missions/s03-two-machine-request-reply.md) | A fixed public request/result stayed the same while B exchanged private `lookup` representations; delivery was separate from explicit execution. | Identity, authority, reachability, durable communication, resource sharing, or a request framework. |
| [S04](../missions/s04-bounded-resource-reference-projection.md) | A fixed public resource/revision selector produced the same bounded projection while B exchanged private layouts; delivery was separate from explicit projection. | Logical/global resource identity, revision history, ownership/provenance, durable sharing, or a resource foundation. |
| [S05](../missions/s05-parallel-working-views-selection.md) | Retained records reconstructed one base, isolated sibling candidates, declared parent/context, and explicit sibling decisions across a fresh process without overwrite or integration. | General continuity, a session runtime, resource/revision semantics, Git/worktrees, merge/reconciliation, or a working-view primitive. |

S05's fresh-process reconstruction is evidence about retained records. It is not general continuity evidence: M00 alone tested a consequence surviving an actual operator replacement.

## Recurrence and non-promotion

The records repeatedly use bounded retained material and make one relationship inspectable without requiring all local operational detail. S03 and S04 additionally preserve one public interaction while receiver-private realization changes; S05 preserves a declared local derivation/decision relationship across a fresh process; M00 preserves one accepted consequence and provenance across operator replacement.

This is a **candidate recurring shape, not a primitive**. The four records do not share a common schema, lifecycle, authority model, component boundary, or substitution contract. In particular, S05 does not establish a public machine interaction or private-realization substitution, and M00 does not establish a generic record processor. Extracting a “record,” “event,” “working view,” “resource,” “session,” or generic request layer would add claims that no specimen required.

Plain retained records were sufficient for S05's bounded divergent-candidate and explicit-decision control. S03 and S04 also did not expose a need for a transport framework, event system, resource database, Git, CAS, session object, generic capability API, or shared helper. This is local negative evidence, not a claim that those mechanisms are universally unnecessary.

## Boundary reading

The evidence keeps the following distinctions useful without adopting them as architecture:

- **Retained representation ↔ reconstruction:** records can bridge a fresh process when the bounded relationship is stated explicitly; whether that bridge is machine continuity remains contextual.
- **Communication ↔ execution:** delivery and a receiver's explicit action remained distinct in S03 and S04.
- **Semantic/public interaction ↔ private realization:** S03 and S04 held a narrow public contract while B-private implementation changed.
- **Base/candidate/decision relationship:** S05 made derivation and local sibling disposition explicit without merge, integration, or session semantics.

None has independently earned a general interface, a runtime owner, or a reusable allocation. The [embodiment map](em-embodiment-map-v0.md) and [boundary hypotheses](em-boundary-hypotheses-v0.md) remain question-bearing lenses, not architecture declarations.

## What would reopen experimentation

A later brief must name the new pressure and why S03–S05 cannot already answer it. Eligible pressure could include:

1. a concrete resource/workflow need that cannot express required reconstruction or substitution with the present plain records;
2. actual concurrent work that makes explicit sibling disposition insufficient and exposes a particular reconciliation relationship;
3. observed project-wide arrival noise after a bounded retained work-context control, creating an attention relationship rather than merely a host-notification concern; or
4. a real multi-machine workflow in which fixed local aliases/provenance cannot distinguish identity, authority, or reachability.

Otherwise, defer. The next valid artifact may be a separately authorized candidate-boundary specification against a real machine-ecology workflow, not another specimen implementation. This review neither adopts the roadmap nor authorizes that artifact.
