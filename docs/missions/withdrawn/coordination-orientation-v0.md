# Coordination Orientation v0 (Withdrawn)

Status: withdrawn before execution; no evidence produced
Owner: Epistemic Machine workshop  
Participant: Continuity Lab

## Withdrawal

This candidate brief is retained as historical design context. Its proposed roadmap prerequisite was never adopted, no execution occurred, and it does not select a future mission or experiment. A future orientation study requires a new human-selected brief.

## Question

Does the Machine Ecology Coordination Roadmap, together with source-local entry material, help fresh EM and CL operators reconstruct a compatible account of current direction and authority without reading the peer repository or treating engineering direction as scientific authority?

This tests orientation, not artifact lineage, synchronization, cognition quality, or architecture.

## Candidate effect

A useful orientation artifact should let fresh operators answer, with source-local citations:

1. What is the EM roadmap's north star, and what is this repository's own current question or orientation?
2. What has actually been established?
3. What is currently chosen engineering direction?
4. What remains hypothetical?
5. Is a roadmap coordination head adopted, and what is the current milestone?
6. What activity, if any, is authorized under the frozen run manifest?
7. What is the next possible gated activity beyond that authorized scope?
8. What remains uncertain?
9. Who owns each relevant decision, and what happens if EM direction conflicts with CL authority?

## Control and comparison

The execution brief must freeze comparable source sets and operator conditions before any run.

- **Control:** source-local orientation at the pre-Stage-1 repository baselines—CL `7858244` and EM `d4e1418`—without the new roadmap/advisory. An execution specification must verify and materialize those source sets without exposing later history.
- **Treatment:** source-local orientation after Stage 1, with EM receiving its roadmap and CL receiving its advisory/crosswalk. The execution specification must freeze the exact post-Stage-1 commits.
- A CL operator reads only CL-authorized sources. An EM operator reads only EM-authorized sources. Neither reads the peer tree.
- Model/provider/runtime, prompts, source byte counts, retries, and incidents must be recorded. Non-comparable executions bound any comparison.

No numeric improvement threshold was preregistered in this withdrawn brief. Before execution, a separately approved execution specification must define scoring and invalidation without using treatment results.

## Required observations

For each operator preserve:

- verbatim answers to the nine questions;
- citations used;
- authority errors, unsupported promotions, and omitted uncertainties;
- sources actually available and read;
- execution metadata required by the CL comparability methodology;
- any request for peer-tree access or reliance on conversation history.

The evidence package and review remain outside the roadmap.

## Success and limits

A treatment run is minimally coherent only if both fresh operators:

- give compatible accounts of the EM roadmap's north star while preserving each repository's own orientation and current question; compatibility does not require identical north stars;
- distinguish evidence, working direction, adopted-head status, candidate experiments, current authorization, and a merely possible next gate;
- attribute EM roadmap ownership, CL epistemic/methodology ownership, and human adoption/activation authority correctly;
- preserve any conflict between EM direction and CL authority for human disposition rather than silently resolving it;
- cite only source-local material.

That result would show bounded reconstructibility under the recorded conditions. A relative improvement claim additionally requires a valid comparable control. It would not prove machine continuity, general semantic interfaces, lineage, event semantics, or a universal architecture.

## Stop conditions

Stop and invalidate or repair the apparatus if:

- the roadmap is treated as scientific authority or build authorization;
- CL advisory material is treated as an EM decision;
- either operator reads the peer repository outside the bus/provided packet boundary;
- treatment information leaks into the control;
- execution begins before a separately approved specification freezes prompts, sources, scoring, and invalidation;
- the roadmap is modified during a run;
- experiment evidence is written into the roadmap.

## Historical activation gate

The withdrawn proposal required an explicitly human-adopted roadmap coordination head plus a new human approval naming the frozen execution specification and allowed model/provider/runtime. Neither occurred.
