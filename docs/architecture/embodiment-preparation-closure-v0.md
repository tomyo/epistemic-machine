# Embodiment Preparation — Closure v0

**Status:** closed preparation phase — historical preparation record; M01 / Habitat-0 is now authorized and implemented within its bounded scope

## Scope completed

Embodiment Preparation established a deliberately small, revisable guide for a possible first local embodiment. It produced:

- the provisional machine / ecology / host responsibility map;
- the Habitat-0 status and deferrals;
- candidate Roadmap v1 and proposed M01 acceptance boundary;
- curated design lineage and bounded prior-art comparisons; and
- a read-only archaeology of the legacy EM repository.

This work chose no runtime, substrate, protocol, identity model, event system, revision store, synchronization mechanism, seed, daemon, or shared foundation.

## Outcome

M01 / Habitat-0 is the current **authorized** bounded embodiment. The preparation phase made its boundary legible; the subsequent human decision adopted Roadmap v1 and authorized M01 without expanding its scope.

The architectural distinctions worth carrying into real use are:

1. host realization, habitat mechanism, and machine responsibility are different surfaces;
2. delivery, materialization, attention, interpretation, adoption, and durable consequence must not be inferred from one another;
3. resource revision can differ from a grouped working consequence; and
4. identity, authority, and current reachability are independent facts.

They are guidance for interpreting real pressure, not requirements to implement mechanisms now.

## Evidence and caution

The legacy repository supports the restraint: it mixed projections, event-like records, polling, local process assumptions, and non-transactional mutation before their ownership and durability contracts were sound. Its retained useful pressure is narrower: a later embodiment may need to distinguish a runtime's contribution, a machine-owned contribution, and accepted incorporation—particularly through interruption or concurrent writers.

That is a future observation target, not an M01 feature.

## Decision resolution

The human adopted Roadmap v1 as the coordination head and separately authorized M01 by name. The workshop now builds only its bounded Habitat-0 realization. Use—not further speculative architecture—should determine whether a newly observed issue is an ordinary defect, an architecture pressure, an isolated uncertainty, or no actionable pressure.
